CUSTOMER PULSE / FORMULA

Simple, explainable, consistent.

({Google × 50%} + {Uber Eats × 30%} + {DoorDash × 20%}) ÷ 5 × 100

Weights reflect an editorial dashboard choice. They're not based on Grill'd policy, and sources do not measure identical customer populations. Missing ratings mean no score for that date.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Review score ≠ store performance.

This site does not know sales, service times, team performance, orders, complaints received internally or official Grill'd audits. Your private shift score is a self-report, not verified operational data.
